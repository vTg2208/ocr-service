## Design Context

### Users

Registry office staff use this interface at a desk to process patta documents efficiently and accurately. Their core workflow is to upload a record, verify extracted fields, locate the corresponding registered parcel, and record a claim while recognizing ambiguity or conflicts.

### Brand Personality

Authoritative, calm, and precise. The interface should create confidence in the record-handling process without feeling bureaucratic, technical, or decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a light institutional interface with restrained cadastral character, emblem-led forest green, green-tinted neutral surfaces, clear data hierarchy, and practical density. Amber and red are reserved for warnings and errors. Avoid generic AI-dashboard patterns, warm beige or rust decoration, oversized decorative typography, excessive monospace text, technical implementation language, redundant cards, and unnecessary explanatory copy.

### Design Principles

1. Keep the current task and next action obvious at every step.
2. Reveal detail progressively: show evidence, warnings, and alternatives when they become relevant.
3. Optimize for repeated staff use with compact, legible forms and predictable placement.
4. Communicate status through clear language, shape, and color rather than color alone.
5. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, comfortable touch targets, and responsive layouts.
